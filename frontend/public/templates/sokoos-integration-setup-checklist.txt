SOKOOS INTEGRATION SETUP CHECKLIST

Use this checklist to plan which business services to connect. Do not write passwords, API keys, access tokens, OAuth secrets, payment credentials, WhatsApp credentials, database credentials, private keys, or any other secret in this document.

Connect and authorize every service only inside Sokoos. For each service you need, make sure you have an authorized business account available, then open Integrations in Sokoos, choose the matching lesson and service, complete the connection there, and confirm its status in Sokoos.

Use one status for each service: Not needed / Ready to connect / Connected in Sokoos / Setup required / Coming soon.
